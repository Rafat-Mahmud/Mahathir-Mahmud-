    How do I get a transcript from a class link?
    1. YouTube: open the video, tap …more under it, choose Show transcript, select all the text and copy it.
    2. Zoom, Teams, Meet recordings: open the recording page, use Transcript or Captions, then copy or download it.
    3. Paste it above. Time stamps are fine and are kept as references in the note.
